WELCOME TO BOMBAE SQUARE

Bombae Square is the cultural wing of 4C, built to celebrate creativity, talent and artistic expression through events, performances and experiences that bring the campus community together.

From the art lanes of Kala Ghoda to the old chawls of Girgaon, we've mapped 40 spots across the city where Mumbai's living culture shows up loudest, and we're inviting you to walk through it with us.
